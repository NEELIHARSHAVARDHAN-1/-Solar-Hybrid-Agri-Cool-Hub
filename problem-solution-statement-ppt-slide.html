Problem & Solution Statement
Problem Statement
Who is Affected
Smallholder farmers & transport operators in rural India face significant post-harvest crop losses due to unmonitored storage conditions during transportation.
Key Challenges
No way to monitor temperature, humidity, or gas levels inside a sealed moving storage unit.
Spoilage is discovered only at the destination — too late to save the produce.
Limitations of Existing Solutions
Manual checks are infrequent, unreliable, and provide no real-time data.
Commercial cold-chain IoT systems are priced for large logistics companies — unaffordable for small farms.
No affordable, solar-capable, connected solution exists for last-mile agricultural transport.
Solution Statement
Proposed Solution
Solar Hybrid Agri Cool Hub — an IoT-powered smart preservation system that actively cools (Peltier module), humidifies (solar-powered), and continuously monitors crop storage conditions.
How It Works
Sensors measure temp, humidity & gas every 15 s → encrypted to cloud → AI alert engine → farmer notified instantly on Android app or web dashboard.
Key Features & Benefits
Real-time alerts (WARNING & CRITICAL) before spoilage occurs — time to act.
Offline resilience — no data lost during connectivity outages; replayed automatically.
Solar-hybrid power reduces electricity costs for rural, off-grid deployments.
Impact: Reduced post-harvest losses → higher crop quality → better farmer income → sustainable food supply chains.
